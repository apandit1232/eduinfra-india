Offer Letter Format

Date: 30 July 2026

Dear Candidate,

We are pleased to offer you the position of Trainee Engineer at our organization. Your starting salary will be as discussed during the interview.

Please note that this is an initial offer. A detailed Appointment Letter containing all rules, regulations, and exact benefits will be provided to you on your date of joining. Kindly sign and return a copy of this letter as a token of your acceptance.

Best Regards,
HR Department

Letter of Intent (LOI) Format

Dear Candidate,

Following your recent interview, we are writing to express our intent to hire you. We were very impressed with your background in Mechanical Engineering and your CAD skills.

This Letter of Intent is subject to the successful completion of your background verification and final management approval. Once cleared, a formal Offer Letter will be issued to you.

Appointment Letter Format

Welcome to the Company!

We are delighted to appoint you to the role of Trainee Engineer. Below are the key terms of your employment:

  • Working Hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Monday to Saturday).
  • Probation Period: You will be on probation for 6 months from the date of joining.
  • Notice Period: Either party can terminate this agreement by giving a 30-day notice.
  • Confidentiality: You are required to maintain strict confidentiality regarding company operations and manufacturing processes.

Please sign the duplicate copy of this letter confirming your agreement to these terms.
