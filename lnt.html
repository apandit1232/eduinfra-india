Larsen & Toubro (L&T) - Corporate Details & Subsidiaries

About Larsen & Toubro (L&T)

Larsen & Toubro (L&T) ek Indian multinational conglomerate hai, jo engineering, construction, manufacturing, aur technology mein global leader hai. Inka focus hamesha high-quality production aur employee safety par rehta hai.

Manufacturing & Industrial Operations

L&T ke heavy engineering aur manufacturing plants mein production ko fast aur error-free banane ke liye method study aur motion economy ke concepts ka kaafi gahrayi se istemal kiya jata hai. Iske alawa, heavy machinery aur engine components ke overhauling ke samay strict industrial safety regulations, jaise ki Factories Act 1948 ke standards, ko poori tarah lagoo kiya jata hai taaki zero-accident work environment maintain rahe.

Wage Structure & Employee Welfare

Ek massive workforce ko manage karne ke liye L&T ka wage structure kafi transparent hai. Industry standards ke mutabiq base pay ke sath-sath, company inflation aur market conditions ko dhyan mein rakhte hue nominal wages aur actual purchasing power (real wages) ke beech ek sahi balance banati hai.

Independent Companies & Subsidiaries

L&T group ke andar kai independent companies operate karti hain jo alag-alag sectors mein expert hain:

L&T Technology Services (LTTS)

Pure-play Engineering Research & Development (ER&D) par focus. Yeh mechanical aur manufacturing engineering ke advanced digitalization solutions provide karti hai.

LTIMindtree

L&T Infotech (LTI) aur Mindtree ke milne se bani yeh Bharat ki leading IT services aur digital consulting company hai.

L&T Finance Holdings

Yeh financial division rural, housing aur infrastructure sectors ke liye specialized loans aur investment services deta hai.

L&T Heavy Engineering

Yeh independent segment global refineries, oil & gas, aur power plants ke liye complex aur critical equipment design aur manufacture karta hai.
